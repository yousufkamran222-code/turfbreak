Turfbreak privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Turfbreak does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics in this version of the game.

What the game stores

  • The game saves your progress on your device only. On the web, this is the browser's local storage. In the Android app, it is the app's own storage.
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Clearing the site data in your browser, or uninstalling the Android app, removes the saved data.

Ads

  • Browser versions of the game that run on game portals, such as CrazyGames, may show ads. Those ads are served by the portal's own software. The portal's privacy policy applies to any data it collects.
  • The Android app shows no ads in this version.

Third parties

This version of the game does not share data with third parties. Game portals may collect data under their own policies when you play on them.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If future versions add analytics, purchases or any other way of collecting data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
